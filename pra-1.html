About GreenLeaf Organics

Founded in 2026, GreenLeaf Organics Pvt. Ltd. partners directly with over 150 small-scale organic farmers to promote regenerative agriculture and bring unadulterated food to urban households.

Why Choose GreenLeaf?

  • NPOP & USDA Organic Certified: Every batch undergoes strict residue-free lab testing.
  • Fair Farmer Pricing: Farmers receive 30% above conventional market rates.
  • Eco-Friendly Packaging: 100% biodegradable paper, cloth, and glass containers.
  • Full Traceability: Scan any product label to view the exact farm origin and harvest date.
